The Dead Media Archive

How much retro hardware would your digital file require to store?

Every file is just a pile of bytes, and every storage device ever built could hold only so many of them. Choose a photo, song, or video and the archive divides its size by each device’s capacity, from modern flash drives back to the punch cards of 1928. Then walk the timeline and see the stack of hardware your file would have needed.
